# AdaptIQ AI

### An AI Learning Companion That Adapts to YOU.

## Project Overview

AdaptIQ AI is an AI-powered adaptive learning platform designed to provide every student with a personalized learning experience. Instead of giving every learner the same explanations and study materials, AdaptIQ AI builds a personalized **Digital Learning Twin** that continuously learns how a student understands, studies, and performs.

The Learning Twin analyzes a student's learning preferences, strengths, weaknesses, confidence, quiz performance, recurring mistakes, and progress. This information allows the platform to continuously adapt the learning experience to the individual student.

Students can interact with AdaptIQ AI through multiple learning methods. They can ask questions directly, upload textbooks, notes, or educational PDFs, and record or provide classroom lecture material. The platform processes this content using AI and transforms it into resources tailored to the student's needs, including simplified explanations, personalized notes, quizzes, flashcards, mind maps, and revision plans.

The platform goes beyond simply answering questions. After a student completes quizzes or learning activities, AdaptIQ AI analyzes their performance and feeds the results back into their Learning Twin. Weak areas, recurring mistakes, and changes in confidence can therefore be identified and used to personalize future learning sessions.

For example, if a student repeatedly struggles with applying formulas, AdaptIQ AI can recognize that pattern and provide more step-by-step explanations, examples, and targeted practice. As the student improves, the Learning Twin is updated, allowing the system to continuously adjust the difficulty, explanation style, and learning resources.

### How AdaptIQ AI Works

The core adaptive learning cycle is:

**LEARN → QUIZ → ANALYZE → UPDATE LEARNING TWIN → PERSONALIZE → LEARN AGAIN**

1. **Student Profile**
   - The student creates their learning profile.
   - AdaptIQ AI establishes their initial Learning Twin.

2. **Learn**
   - Students ask questions, upload educational PDFs, or provide lecture material.
   - AI analyzes the learning content and student context.

3. **Personalize**
   - The platform generates explanations and resources according to the student's learning preferences, strengths, weaknesses, and confidence.

4. **Practice**
   - Students can generate quizzes and practice questions based on the material they are studying.

5. **Analyze**
   - Quiz results are analyzed to identify knowledge gaps, recurring mistakes, and topic-level performance.

6. **Update**
   - The Learning Twin evolves based on the student's latest performance.

7. **Adapt**
   - Future explanations, questions, and learning resources become increasingly personalized.

### What Makes AdaptIQ AI Different?

Most AI educational tools primarily adapt to the **question** a student asks.

AdaptIQ AI is designed to adapt to the **student**.

Instead of treating every learner the same, the platform builds an evolving understanding of the individual learner and uses that information throughout their learning journey.

The goal is not simply to provide answers, but to understand **how the student learns, where they struggle, and how they can improve**.

### Technology

AdaptIQ AI is built using a modular full-stack architecture:

- **Frontend:** Next.js, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS
- **Backend:** Python, FastAPI
- **AI:** Large Language Models through an API-based AI layer
- **Database:** PostgreSQL / Supabase
- **Document Processing:** PDF text extraction and retrieval
- **Lecture Processing:** Speech-to-text and AI analysis
- **Authentication:** Supabase Authentication
- **Development:** Git, GitHub, and AI-assisted development tools

The architecture separates the frontend, backend, AI services, personalization logic, and database so that the platform can be expanded with additional AI models and learning features in the future.

### Vision

AdaptIQ AI aims to make personalized education accessible to every student.

Every learner has different strengths, weaknesses, learning preferences, and challenges. Instead of forcing students to adapt to a fixed learning system, AdaptIQ AI aims to create a system that **adapts to them**.

Our vision is to build an intelligent learning companion that grows alongside the student and continuously helps them understand better, practice smarter, identify weaknesses earlier, and make meaningful progress.

> **AdaptIQ AI — Most AI tutors adapt to the question. AdaptIQ AI adapts to the student.**
